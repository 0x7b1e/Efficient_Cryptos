# DEX economic principles

Generate hypotheses from observable on-chain market behavior: swap flow,
liquidity withdrawal, volume relative to liquidity, price impact, trader
activity, pool stress, and DEX/CEX lead-lag when a CEX field is explicitly
present.  Do not claim wallet or buy/sell information exists when the input
column is NaN for the OHLCV baseline.

Every factor must state why the behavior could persist for the next 30
minutes, identify its direction, and describe failure modes such as wash
trading, arbitrage, MEV, stale pools, low liquidity, and token migration.
