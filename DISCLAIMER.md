## Risks of trading cryptocurrency derivatives

- **You can lose more than you expect.** Perpetual futures are leveraged products. Price moves, liquidations, funding payments and fees can wipe out your entire margin quickly.
- **Crypto markets are extremely volatile.** Prices can gap, liquidity can disappear, and exchanges can halt trading, change rules, delist contracts or fail outright.
- **Past performance does not predict future results.** Backtests, shadow replays and Testnet runs are simulations. They do not capture real slippage, order-book impact, latency, partial fills, rejections, outages or funding conditions, and results from them are subject to overfitting and multiple-testing bias.
- **LLM-generated factors are unverified.** Factors produced by large language models may be meaningless, overfitted, or contain subtle look-ahead bugs despite the provided audits.
- **Software can fail.** This code may contain bugs. Network failures, API changes, clock drift, misconfiguration or exchange-side issues can cause unintended orders, missed stop-losses or positions you did not plan for.
- **Security is your responsibility.** Protect your API keys, restrict their permissions (never enable withdrawals), and use IP allow-lists.
