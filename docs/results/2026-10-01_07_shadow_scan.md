# Factor shadow scan, 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-07 (historical record)

> The original comes from Developer_Crypto commit `908930e`; its conclusions are kept here as they were.
> The predictions used in this scan were exported **before** the Predict_Cryptos backtest accounting fix (PR #1). For factors using `share`, `rank()` or `zscore()`, the predictions contain future information (see the audit script in docs/02). With only one week of data, they also cannot be treated as out-of-sample conclusions.

## Setup

Replayed the 441 selected factors in `selected_factor/crypto_selected_dpsk.jsonl` across six rebalancing periods: 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h, 12h and 24h:
- intraday risk control uses a 1-minute TWAP;
- 3x gross leverage, initial equity 10,000;
- 5 bps fees, 1 bp slippage;
- the data directory had no funding parquet files for these symbols, so `total_funding` is 0.

## Conclusions

The most robust factor across all six periods was `volatility_crypto_templates_10k_v1_0052_0004`: positive returns in all six periods, final state `LIVE` in all of them, mean return about +17.07%, median Sharpe about 14.50, worst drawdown about −6.91%.

| Rebalancing period | Return | Sharpe | Max drawdown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1h | +15.79% | 13.37 | -6.91% |
| 2h | +14.25% | 12.57 | -3.89% |
| 4h | +20.16% | 16.94 | -5.46% |
| 8h | +16.85% | 12.99 | -6.91% |
| 12h | +23.57% | 19.03 | -5.33% |
| 24h | +19.75% | 16.02 | -5.39% |

Other results:
- **Second tier**: `volatility_crypto_templates_10k_v1_0075_0008`, `liquidity_crypto_templates_10k_v1_liquidity_0097_3`, `volatility_crypto_templates_10k_v1_0079_006`.
- **Highest single-period return**: `volume_crypto_templates_10k_v1_0004_0005` reached +52.88% at 4h, but ended in `SHADOW` and was less stable across periods than the top factor.
- **`liquidity_crypto_templates_10k_v1_liquidity_0041_5`, used live at the time**: returns from 1h to 24h were +3.17%, −25.73%, +15.42%, −13.01%, −7.23% and −7.04%; all six periods ended in `SHADOW`.

Recommendation: validate the top factor on later dates before considering switching the live factor.

```bash
  --fee-bps 5 --slippage-bps 1 --mark-minutes 1

```
